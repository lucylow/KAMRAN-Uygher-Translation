# KAMRAN Privacy Policy Draft

**Effective date:** 2026-08-21

KAMRAN Translate is a local-first Uyghur–Chinese translation companion. This draft describes the current MVP behavior and must be reviewed and published at a stable public URL before App Store submission. It is not legal advice.

## Information stored on the device

The current MVP stores translation history, favorite markers, language and accessibility preferences, onboarding completion, and cultural-learning progress locally on the user’s device. This information is used only to provide the app’s local features and is not required to create an account.

## Microphone and camera access

KAMRAN may request microphone access when the user chooses voice translation. It may request camera or photo-library access when the user chooses camera/OCR translation. Access is requested for the selected feature, and users can deny or later revoke permission in iOS Settings. The app should not claim that audio or images remain local if a future production recognition service uploads them for processing.

## Network services

This MVP uses deterministic local service boundaries for core demonstrations. Before release, the owner must review every enabled production integration and update this policy and App Store Connect App Privacy answers if text, audio, images, diagnostics, analytics, authentication, or other data leaves the device.

## Sharing and sale of information

KAMRAN does not sell the locally stored MVP history, favorites, settings, or learning progress. Any future analytics, crash reporting, cloud synchronization, or AI provider must be added to this policy before activation.

## Data deletion

Users can clear translation history and reset cultural-learning progress from the app’s Settings surfaces. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data according to iOS behavior. If a future account or server sync is introduced, provide an account deletion process and revise this section.

## Children’s privacy

KAMRAN is an educational language tool and is not intentionally directed at collecting personal information from children. The final age-rating and audience choices must be reviewed against the actual production content and any future online services.

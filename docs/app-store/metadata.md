# KAMRAN App Store Submission Metadata

## App name

**KAMRAN Translate**

## Subtitle

**Uyghur–Chinese translation**

## Promotional text

Translate text, voice, and camera moments while learning the language and culture behind every conversation.

## Description

KAMRAN is a focused Uyghur–Chinese translation companion for everyday conversations. Translate written phrases, review saved history, listen to spoken results, and explore cultural learning cards that add context to the words you use.

The app includes text translation with language-direction and regional variant context, voice capture and playback states, native camera capture with OCR review flows, persistent local history and favorites, and cultural lessons covering music, poetry, cuisine, and writing.

KAMRAN is designed for calm, one-handed use. Clear review states, bilingual labels, reduced-motion support, large touch targets, and accessible status announcements help users stay oriented during translation.

## Keywords

Uyghur, Chinese, translation, language, voice, OCR, camera, phrases, culture, bilingual

## App Review notes

KAMRAN can be reviewed without an account or external credentials. The core text translation, history, favorites, voice-state, native camera capture/OCR review, and cultural-learning flows are available from the main tabs. Camera permission and capture are native in the iOS build; text recognition and translation remain deterministic local fallback services until the production recognition service is connected.

To review the main flows, open Translate for text translation, use the voice controls to inspect capture and playback states, open OCR from Home to inspect camera/gallery review, open History to inspect saved entries and favorites, and open Learn to inspect cultural cards and completion persistence. Settings contains local preference, reduced-motion, progress-reset, and accessibility surfaces.

## Age rating guidance

The app contains educational language and cultural content with no user-generated social feed, purchases, gambling, advertising, or mature content. Confirm the final rating answers in App Store Connect against the exact production build and content.

## Privacy disclosure preparation

The current MVP stores translation history, favorites, settings, onboarding completion, and cultural progress locally on the device using AsyncStorage. No account is required for the core flows. Update the App Privacy answers if production integrations later transmit text, audio, images, diagnostics, analytics, or account data to a service.
